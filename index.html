Find the spot everyone else misses.

Seven boards a day. Every board has a few rules. Drop one pin where all of them hold, as far from the crowd as you dare.

  1. 1Read the rules. Somewhere on the board every rule is true at once. That area is hidden.
  2. 2Tap to place your pin, drag to adjust, then drop it. Outside the area scores 0.
  3. 3Quiet corners score more. Tapping where most people tap scores almost nothing.

30 seconds per board. Turn on Bold for 1.5× points, but a miss costs 40.
